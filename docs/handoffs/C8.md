# C8 handoff

**Price-feed ADR:** [0019](../adr/0019-rebalancer-price-and-execution.md).
**Runbook:** `ops/runbook.md` → Rebalancer.

## Done

- **`services/apps/rebalancer`**: one loop per escrow (spec 6.7). It signs with the escrow's
  `rebalancer` key through the same KMS path as C5.
  - That path is now the `@chain/signer` package, extracted from the quote service. C5 re-exports
    it, so C5's API and tests are unchanged.
  - The new `kmsAccount` signs transactions.
- **Required liquid amount.** It is computed from the ledger at `latest` and at `finalized` (the
  larger of each), plus the booking projection: claimables, disputed and pending amounts, plus the
  principal of bookings with check-in within 14 days (including stays in progress and bookings
  awaiting settlement), floored at `MIN_BUFFER_BPS`.
  - It is published to `rebalancer.required_liquid` and is in every decision row.
  - C6's INV-5 computes the same figure independently with the same function.
- **Decisions** (`src/policy.ts`, one pure function; every check is recorded in the log):
  - redeem everything withdrawable when market liquidity is below 5x our position;
  - redeem the gap when idle is below the required amount, whatever the price;
  - deploy only if every gate passes:
    - no loss debt and no shortfall;
    - price fresh and within ±1%, with the sequencer up and past its grace period;
    - reserve at or above the floor;
    - projection current;
    - at least 500 USDC, from finalised idle only;
    - within the buffer and `maxDeployBps`;
    - 20x market liquidity after the move;
    - and it must pass an exact replica of `LedgerLib.deploy`;
  - never redeem because of the price.
- **Execution** (`src/executor.ts`):
  - simulate before every move;
  - write the signed transaction ahead of broadcast;
  - one transaction in flight per escrow;
  - a stuck transaction gets the same call at the same nonce with fees +12.5%, or a same-nonce
    cancel if the decision changed;
  - a revert starts a cooldown (10 min, doubling to 6 h);
  - one process per escrow (advisory lock);
  - **dry run by default**; only `DRY_RUN=false` sends.
- **Decision log** (`rebalancer.decisions`): every cycle's snapshot, checks, decision, outcome and
  transaction hash.
- **Price source** (ADR 0019): Chainlink USDC/USD on Base, `0x7e86…bc6B`, 24 h heartbeat, stale
  after 90,000 s. Sequencer uptime feed `0xBCF8…6433` with a 3,600 s grace period. Both verified
  on-chain, and the reader was checked against live Base.

## Threshold recommendation (20x deploy gate, 5x exit)

**Data:** 90 days, from 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-29, 181 samples at 12 h spacing. The source is
archive state on Base mainnet (`Pool.getVirtualUnderlyingBalance(USDC)`, aUSDC and variable-debt
supply). The script is `services/apps/rebalancer/scripts/liquidity-history.sh`, and the data is in
`docs/spikes/c8-liquidity/`.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Available USDC | min 16.7M (2026-08-17, utilisation 90.3%), p5 17.6M, median 22.7M, max 44.1M |
| Utilisation | 77.4% to 90.7%, median 87.5% |
| Largest fall in available liquidity | 41.5% within 12 h, 47.7% within 24 h |

| Our position | 20x needs | Samples below 20x | 5x needs | Samples below 5x |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100k | 2M | 0% | 0.5M | 0% |
| 500k | 10M | 0% | 2.5M | 0% |
| 1M | 20M | 30% | 5M | 0% |
| 2M | 40M | 98% | 10M | 0% |

**Recommendation: keep 20x and 5x.**
- **At pilot scale these gates never bind.** Positions stay far below the roughly 880k (1/20 of the
  p5) at which the 20x gate starts to bite.
- **The margin covers the worst swing seen.** Going from the 20x deploy gate to the 5x exit needs a
  75% fall in available liquidity, and falling from 5x to below our own position needs another 80%.
  Both exceed the largest 24 h fall observed (48%).
- **Raise the deploy multiple, not the exit, if positions ever exceed about 800k.** Lowering 20x
  would deploy into the same pool with thinner exit room. The exit multiple is the backstop and
  stays at 5x.

**Caveat:** 12 h sampling misses intraday spikes. Before mainnet, re-run at 1 h spacing with a
keyed RPC.

## Deviations from spec

- **Deployable idle is `min(idle, idle at the finalized block)`, not a projection sum.** It needs no
  projection freshness to be correct, and it also excludes non-deposit inflows until they are
  finalised. It is the more conservative reading of "only finalised deposits count".
- **The testnet liquidity source is the mock vault's withdraw limit.** It stands in for "market
  liquidity"; see ADR 0019 §2.

## Spec concerns

- **The liquidity-exit rule can redeem during a depeg.** Spec 6.7's "redeem all below 5x" applies
  regardless of price, and nothing forbids it, because it is liquidity-driven, not price-driven. But
  if a crunch and a depeg coincide, it locks in the depeg loss. I kept the spec's rule. Worth an
  explicit owner decision.
- **The ±1% gate relies on the feed's deviation trigger.** I could not read the trigger (Chainlink's
  docs are blocked here). With a 24 h heartbeat, a slow depeg could go unseen for up to a day if the
  trigger were at or above 1%. Confirm it before mainnet (ADR 0019 §1).

## Interfaces changed

- **New package `@chain/signer`**, extracted from the quote service. C5's `src/signer.ts`
  re-exports it, with no behaviour change; its 77 unit tests and 5 Anvil tests pass.
- **The indexer test harness's `book()`** takes an optional `escrow`. Test-only, additive.
- **New `rebalancer` schema:** `decisions`, `pending_tx`, `cooldowns`, `required_liquid`.
