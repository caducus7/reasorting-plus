### Ghost model (what "spec formula" means here)

- **accrue** (6.1 + ADR 0009 high-water mark + ADR 0010 §4 threshold of 1 USDC):
  - Gains first repay `lossDebt`, then go to `acc += d*1e18/open`, or to the reserve when
    `open == 0`.
  - A dip never lowers `lastAssets`.
  - A shortfall of at least 1 USDC opens the observation; the model runs this before every escrow
    call and rolls it back if the call reverts.
- **Settlement** (4.3, 4.4, 7):
  - refund = `ceilDiv(p*bps, 1e4)`; fee = floor of retained × `feeBps`; owner takes the rest.
  - `y = p*(acc − accAtDeposit)/1e18`, vested only on delivered settlement or `guestBps == 0` (D3).
  - Yield is deferred while `lossDebt > 0`.
  - Disputes: partial settlement at open, yield crystallised at open.
- **Claims** (4.5, ADR 0007/0009/0010):
  - paid = `min(owed, idle + vault.maxWithdraw)`.
  - While `lossDebt > 0` or a shortfall of at least 1 USDC is observed, only the guest bucket is
    paid; owner-only and fee-only callers must revert.
  - Deferred yield is released on claim once no loss is active.
- **Loss recognition** (6.4, ADR 0010 §5): absorbs reserve, then the owner bucket, then the owner's
  deferred yield, then `lossDebt`. Checked against `LossRecognised`.
- **Timelocks** (3.4, ADR 0007 §5): fee, arbitrator and fee recipient are pure functions of time,
  with promote-on-propose. The arbitrator is snapshotted at deposit; the fee recipient is read at
  settlement.
- **Freeze** (ADR 0007/0011): 30-day budget, anyone may unfreeze once it is used, and frozen time
  after `openDispute` extends the dispute deadline.

The `afterInvariant` liveness drain runs at the end of every run:
1. Lift the blacklist and the vault limit.
2. The guardian unfreezes everything frozen.
3. Warp past every `GRACE` and dispute deadline.
4. Recognise any loss and top up `lossDebt`.
5. `settle` or `resolveByDefault` every booking; each must succeed.
6. Everyone claims, guests first.

It then asserts:
- every booking is SETTLED
- every guest has received exactly what was credited to them
- fee-recipient and owner unpaid amounts are at most the unbooked sub-threshold shortfall (ADR 0010 §4)

## Not done

- `forge coverage` was not run for this suite (it compiles unoptimised). Line and branch coverage of
  the escrow by C9 is unmeasured; path coverage is shown by `C9HandlerCoverage` instead.

  - an ERC-1271 quote signer, and `setQuoteSigner` rotation invalidating old quotes
  - reentrancy (the token has no hooks)
  - more than one owner escrow in the fuzz (the second escrow is used only for replay)

  deferred yield, pending-yield release. The CI campaign (100,000 calls per invariant) reaches them
  more often, but I have no per-run counts for it.

## Failures and findings

Each has a minimal reproduction, the spec text it violates, and my judgement of whether the code
or the spec is wrong.

### F1: `pauseDeposits` / `unpauseDeposits` do not run `accrue()`

- **Reproduction** (shrunk by forge):
  1. `attackerDonate(x > 0)` (any gain)
  2. `guardianPause(true, false)`
  3. `lastAssets` and `shortfallSince` are unchanged.

  The same happens with `unpauseDeposits`.
- **Other functions:** a direct probe showed every other state-changing function accrues:
  - all owner setters, `proposeFeeBps`, `proposeArbitrator`, `proposeReserveWithdrawal`
  - `freezeBooking`, `unfreezeBooking`
  - `claim`, `observeShortfall`
- **Violates:** spec 6.1 ("`accrue()`: first line of every state-changing function") and
  CLAUDE.md rule 4. The C1 handoff says rule 4 holds for every function except deposit's guard 5,
  so this contradicts the handoff too.
- **Impact:** none observed. Pausing does not change open principal, so the next accruing call
  distributes the same gain. The ghost is re-synced and all ledger invariants still pass.
- **Judgement:** code and spec disagree literally. I think the **code's behaviour is the better
  one**: an emergency pause should not depend on the vault being priceable (see F3). The spec should
  carve out pause/unpause, recorded in an ADR, or the code should accrue. Project owner decides.

### F2: a deploy into a donated, empty ERC-4626 vault destroys escrow value (property 10)

- **Test:** `C9AdversarialEmptyVault.test_P10_deployIntoDonatedEmptyVault_losesValue_ADR0008`.
- **Reproduction** (vault with no LP):
  1. The attacker transfers 1,000 USDC to the vault.
  2. The rebalancer calls `deploy(1,999e6)`.
  3. The escrow gets 1 share worth 1,499.5 USDC and **loses 499.5 USDC (499,500,000 atomic)** on one
     call.
- **Why the escrow allows it:** its only guard is "shares > 0" (`VaultMintedNoShares`).
- **What happens next:** the loss is then recognised and absorbed by the reserve and the owner. An
  attacker who first mints 1 wei of shares captures part of it: the classic inflation attack.
- **Fuzz run:** the same mechanism caused my first fuzz failures (owner short 0.34 USDC; later
  shortfalls of 1.6 to 13.8 USDC surviving the drain, because each later vault withdrawal burns an
  expensive share). The fuzz vault is now seeded with a 1M USDC LP, as the live Aave wrapper would
  be.
- **Violates:** property 10 ("a compromised rebalancer cannot reduce any account's total
  entitlement") and spec 6.3 ("cannot extract funds"). No attacker is needed on the rebalancer
  side: an honest deploy into a front-run fresh vault loses the same way.
- **Judgement:** a spec/ADR gap. ADR 0008 names the risk and assigns it to C4. Recommend one of:
  - a value check in `deploy` (for example `previewRedeem(minted) >= assets - tolerance`)
  - the factory rejecting vaults with a negligible `totalSupply` or no decimals offset

  Low severity for production if C4 confirms the Aave wrapper is never empty.

### F3: with the vault's views reverting, nothing can settle (property 11)

- **Test:** `test_settleWithBrokenVault_property11`.
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Deposit 10,000 and `deploy(5,000e6)`.
  2. `vault.setBricked(true)`, so `totalAssets` / `previewRedeem` revert.
  3. Warp past `checkOut + GRACE`.
  4. `settle(id)` reverts `Bricked()`.

  Every accrue-first function (cancel, settle, dispute, freeze, claim) is stuck the same way, even
  though settle only credits and 5,000 USDC sits idle.
- **Violates:** property 11 ("settle ... always eventually callable") while the adapter is down.
- **Judgement:** the spec is incomplete, not a code bug. Spec 6.1 requires `accrue()` first and says
  nothing about an adapter that reverts (Aave pause or freeze, a wrapper upgrade gone wrong).
  Suggest a spec rule, for example: `accrue()` treats a reverting vault view as "no change" (or as a
  shortfall), so credit-only transitions and idle-funded guest claims proceed.

### F4: INV-1 as written breaks by 1 atomic unit after an honest deploy

- **Test:** `test_INV1_asWritten_breaksOnHonestDeployRounding_spec10_4`.
- **Reproduction:**
  1. A 1M-USDC vault gains 370,000.000003 (share price ≈ 1.37).
  2. A booking of 10,000.000001 is deposited.
  3. `deploy(7,777.777777)` leaves assets = 10,000.000000 < open principal 10,000.000001.
- **Violates:** spec 10.4 INV-1 ("no warning band ... any breach is a page"). It is never recognised
  (below `MIN_LOSS`).
- **Judgement:** spec. ERC-4626 rounding (ADR 0008) makes exact INV-1 unattainable. The ADR
  0009-adjusted form (`assets + lossDebt + unrecognised shortfall >= liabilities`) holds in every
  CI run. C6's INV-1 monitor needs either that form or a sub-`MIN_LOSS` band, or it will page on
  dust.

### F5: sub-threshold dust can land on the fee recipient (or a late guest), not the owner

- **Where it surfaced:** the default-profile `afterInvariant` run failed with
  `fee recipient not paid in full after drain: 1 != 0` (8 invariants reported it, because the drain
  is shared).
- **Mechanism:**
  1. Vault rounding creates a shortfall of k < 1 USDC. It is not observed or gated (ADR 0010 §4).
  2. The owner claims the whole owner bucket.
  3. Whoever claims last is short by k. In the drain that was the fee recipient; in live use it can
     be a guest.
- **Violates:** spec 2 ("Loss absorption: Owner ... The platform is never a backstop") and 6.4
  (guests first). It is within ADR 0010 §4's accepted residual risk ("guests can be short by at
  most that amount").
- **Judgement:** accepted by ADR, but not "absorbed by the owner" as spec 2 says. The drain
  assertion was changed to the ADR's bound:
  - guests paid in full
  - fee plus owner unpaid ≤ the unbooked shortfall, and < 1 USDC

  This is not a weakening of the spec as amended. It is recorded here so the project owner can
  decide whether owner claims should keep a dust reserve.

## Deviations from spec

None. The suite asserts the spec as amended by ADRs 0001 to 0011. Where a property had to follow an
ADR rather than the base text, I say so: INV-1/INV-2 are loss-adjusted per ADR 0009, and the drain
bound follows ADR 0010 §4. INV-1 as written is still asserted before vault exposure, and its breach

## Spec concerns

- F1 to F5 above.
- **Loss window starts at observation.** The window starts when something calls `accrue`, not when
  the vault drops. `test_lossExactlyAtConfirmationWindowBoundary` pins `shortfallSince` to the
  `observeShortfall` call. This agrees with C2's "observation latency" concern: C6 or C8 must poke
  `observeShortfall()`.
- **Deploy caps:** with `maxDeployBps <= 9,000` and no shortfall, the 10% buffer check can only fail
  when the deploy cap also fails. `BufferBreached` is effectively reachable only in combination, so
  the spec's order of the two checks matters for which error a caller sees. I did not assert a
  specific error there.

## Interface ambiguities resolved

1. **Missing functions and getters.** `IEscrowFactory` has no `approveOwner` or
   `proposeFeeRecipient`, and `IEscrow` has no ledger getters (`lossDebt`, `reserve`,
   `accYieldPerUnit`, …). I declared minimal interfaces (`IEscrowViews`, `IFactoryAdmin` in
   `C9Base.sol`) from the **published ABI JSON** (`contracts/abi/*.json`, signatures only). That is
   outside the forbidden paths and is the "published ABI" the brief depends on.
2. **Spec 13 constants** are not exposed on-chain. I used the spec values: GRACE 72h, DISPUTE_WINDOW
   14d, LOSS window 6h, MAX_FREEZE 30d, MIN_LOSS 1 USDC (ADR 0010), MIN_BUFFER 1,000, MAX_NIGHTS 60.
   All boundary tests passed with them.
3. **Guard 5 vs accrue.** Guard 5 runs before `accrue()` in spec order, so a deposit that arrives
   with `lossDebt > 0` which accrual would repay is EITHER. Every other deposit predicate is exact.
4. **Deposits during an observed shortfall** without debt: predicted to succeed (spec 4.2 guard 5
   names only `lossDebt`). The contract agrees.
5. **Pending-yield release gate.** Modelled as "no `lossDebt` and no shortfall of at least 1 USDC"
   (ADR 0010 §3 "no loss active"). The contract agrees; there were no ledger mismatches.
6. **Shortfall observation.** Starts at the first accrue that sees at least 1 USDC and clears below
   it; recognition is allowed at exactly `since + 6h`. The targeted test confirms this; the exact
   boundary is EITHER in the fuzz.
7. **`frozenTotal` on unfreeze.** Accepted as either elapsed or capped at 30 days. Observed: capped
   (18 times in the driver). The dispute deadline uses the capped value and matched the ghost
   throughout.
8. **Permissionless unfreeze at exactly 30 days.** Allowed ("once the budget is used"). Confirmed in
   `test_freezeBudget_*`.
9. **`resolve` after the dispute deadline** (spec 7 does not forbid it): EITHER.
10. **"Deployed" in the spec 6.3 cap.** Measured before or after vault rounding: EITHER within 2
    atomic units. `redeem` above `maxWithdraw`: EITHER. `fundReserve(0)`, `topUpLoss(0)`, reserve
    proposals: EITHER.
11. **`YieldAccrued.gain`** includes the `toReserve` part (ADR 0010 wording). The sum of events
    equals the ghost's distributed gains in every run.
12. **Blacklisted claimant with nothing payable:** EITHER (it depends on whether a 0 transfer is
    attempted).
13. **Actors are disjoint.** Bucket ordering on a partial claim by a multi-bucket caller is not
    specified and not tested.

Every EITHER is listed above; nowhere else is a prediction lenient.

## Blocked

Nothing blocks C9. Decisions for the project owner:
1. F1: carve pause/unpause out of "accrue first" (ADR), or make them accrue.
2. F2: add a deploy value/slippage check, or have C4 or the factory guarantee a non-empty vault or a
   decimals offset.
3. F3: define `accrue()` behaviour when the vault's views revert.
4. F4: give INV-1 a band (or the ADR 0009 form) for C6's monitor.
5. F5: whether sub-threshold dust may fall on the fee recipient or a late guest.
